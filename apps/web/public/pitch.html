How you get paid

Two ways money comes to you.

Simple as that. One from your fans, one from a shared pot — and both lean your way.

Fans pay you → 100% yours

When a fan sends you €1, you get €1. We only keep 2 cents to pay the bank. That's it.

A slice of the shared pot

Every year the platform shares its spare money with artists. The more people grab your music, the bigger your slice.

Keeping it honest

You can't game what pays nothing to game.

Passive listening earns nothing

The classic bot-farm trick simply has no target here.

Deliberate acts only

Units come from downloads and fan money. Paid needs a real card.

Pattern detection (building)

Batch accounts, even intervals, network clusters, reciprocal rings.

Humans decide. And you get to appeal.

Nothing is docked silently by an algorithm. Anomalies are flagged for a human to review, you're told, and there's a right of appeal. We'd rather miss something than punish someone who did nothing wrong.

On copyright

Releases are protected. Mixes are the whole point.

Original tracks & releases

Every file is fingerprinted on upload. Claiming a work that isn't yours starts with a warning, then suspension, then removal. That protection is what makes a release here mean something.

Mixes & sets

Built from other people's records by design — nobody's pretending otherwise. Add a tracklist and tag any artist on the platform: their name links back to their channel. Crediting sends listeners straight to the makers.

The tracklist isn't a compliance chore — it's how the scene lifts itself.

Independent by design

Real, tested export

Releases, archive, analytics, fan records — all exportable, tested regularly.

Finnish servers

Its own infrastructure in Helsinki — Nebula the strong hosting candidate.

Anonymous by default

You stay because it's good — not because leaving would cost you everything.

The guarantee

It can never be quietly turned against you.

No secret version

AGPL covers network use — so anyone running tahti.live, us included, must publish the exact source they run. There is no hidden build.

The escape hatch is real

If whoever runs it ever betrays the mission, the code and your data are the commons' — the community can fork it and run it themselves.

If it's ever…

Prices creep, or a paid tier appears

→ fork it and run it your way.

Ads or data-selling slip in

→ the constitution forbids it — and the public source means it can't hide.

The org is sold, or folds

→ the platform lives on; export everything.

A feature you rely on is removed

→ keep it alive in a fork.

There is no version of this where you're trapped.

Honest risks

Staying small

A quiet room helps nobody.
What we do: Start with communities like this one — a scene that already talks beats scattered signups.

Funding timing

Grants are applications, not guarantees.
What we do: If money comes slower, development slows — the promises don't change, because they're constitutional.

A small team

Small teams are fragile.
What we do: Succession plan and artist-majority board written in from the start, not bolted on.
